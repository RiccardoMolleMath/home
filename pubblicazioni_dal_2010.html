Research Interests

My research activity concerns nonlinear Partial Differential Equations and systems of elliptic type, coming from physic and geometrical problems.
The methods used are typical of nonlinear analysis: min-max methods, concentration-compactness, Lusternik-Schnirelmann category, degree theory, barycenter maps, minimization methods.

Selected topics

  • prescribed mass problems;
  • Schrödinger-Poisson systems;
  • Choquard equations;
  • the Bahri-Lions conjecture;
  • existence of infinitely many solutions for nonlinear Schrödinger equations;
  • analysis of the Fučík spectrum;
  • critical and supercritical nonlinearities in bounded and unbonded domains;
  • variational inequalities.
